Information collected

Details you provide through the contact form (name, email, phone, project details) and your email address if you subscribe in the footer.

How it's used

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare proposals.
  • To send updates, if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • To improve this website.

Sharing

Enquiries sent through the contact form may be delivered to us by WhatsApp (a Meta service) and email. Your personal data is never sold. It is only shared with service providers needed to operate the site (such as email and hosting), under appropriate confidentiality terms.

Retention & your rights

Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed. You may request access, correction or deletion by getting in touch.

Browser storage

This site may store small amounts of data in your browser (for example form drafts and sign-ups) to make features work. No advertising trackers are used.

Contact

Questions? Email support@one47.online or use the contact page.
