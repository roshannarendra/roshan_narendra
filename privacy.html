Who we are

ONE47 is a brand, design and motion studio based in Gurgaon, Haryana, India. This policy explains how this coming-soon website handles your information. For questions, contact us at support@one47.online.

Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you enter it in the “Notify Me” form so we can tell you when we launch.
  • Messages you send us — if you email or call us, we keep what you share so we can reply.
  • Technical data — like any website, your browser sends your IP address and device details to our hosting provider and to the third-party services listed below when the page loads.

How we use it

  • To notify you about our launch, if you asked us to.
  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Third-party services

This page loads resources from other providers, which means they can see your IP address when your browser requests them:

  • Google Fonts (Inter typeface)
  • OnlineWebFonts (display typeface)
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (Font Awesome icons)
  • Amazon CloudFront (background video)
  • GitHub Pages or the host serving this site

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this page.

Cookies & storage

This page does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

How long we keep it

We keep launch-notification emails until we have notified you or you ask us to delete them. Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop launch emails, by writing to support@one47.online. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) or the GDPR, and we will respect them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website grows. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date below.
