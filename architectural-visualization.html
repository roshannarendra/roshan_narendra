Architectural Visualization FAQ

What is architectural visualization?

Architectural visualization is the creation of realistic 3D images and animations of buildings and interiors from plans, used for design review and property marketing.

Can you render from architects' CAD drawings?

Yes. We work from floor plans, elevations, CAD files and mood references.

Do you create walkthrough videos as well as stills?

Yes. We create animated walkthroughs and fly-through videos for presentations and social campaigns.

Can you also design the marketing material?

Yes. We can design brochures, ads and social content around the renders so the campaign looks unified.
