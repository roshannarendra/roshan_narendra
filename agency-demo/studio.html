Our Story

Founded on a simple idea

Norra started as a two-person freelance partnership and grew into a full studio without losing the habits that made the early work good: short feedback loops, direct access to the people doing the work, and a refusal to hand off a project to someone who wasn't in the room for the strategy.

Today we work with founders, marketing leads, and product teams who need a partner that can move from brand strategy through to a shipped website — without three different vendors in between.
