Frequently asked questions

What services does ONE47 offer?

We offer 3D animation and product visualization, motion design, brand identity design, architectural visualization, Shopify development and Meta Ads management — creative and commercial work under one roof.

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

We are based in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, India, and work with brands, agencies and businesses around the world remotely.

Can you handle both design and marketing for my store?

Yes. We build the brand, create the visuals, set up the Shopify store and run Meta Ads, so everything is aligned and measured.

What tools do you use?

Autodesk Maya, Blender, ZBrush, Keyshot, 3DS Max, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Shopify, Meta Ads and Google Analytics.

How do I start a project?

Use the contact page to send a short brief with your goals, timeline and references. We will come back with a suggested scope and next steps.
