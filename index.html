// About the studio //

Full-service 3D, motion, branding & Shopify studio

ONE47 is a creative and commercial studio based in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, serving brands worldwide. We combine 3D animation and product visualization, motion design, brand identity, architectural visualization, Shopify development and Meta Ads management under one roof, so the visuals that make people look are connected to the store and campaigns that make them buy.

With 4+ years of experience across fintech, enterprise security, e-commerce, real estate and fashion, we work in 13+ professional tools and have delivered for 10+ brands and agencies. Our approach is simple: clear strategy, premium craft and measurable results.

Whether you need a cinematic product animation, a brand that looks as good as your product, or a Shopify store with ads that return on spend, our team can take your project from idea to launch. Tell us about your project.

Frequently asked questions

What services does ONE47 offer?

We offer 3D animation and product visualization, motion design, brand identity design, architectural visualization, Shopify development and Meta Ads management — creative and commercial work under one roof.

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

We are based in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, India, and work with brands, agencies and businesses around the world remotely.

Can you handle both design and marketing for my store?

Yes. We build the brand, create the visuals, set up the Shopify store and run Meta Ads, so everything is aligned and measured.

What tools do you use?

Autodesk Maya, Blender, ZBrush, Keyshot, 3DS Max, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Shopify, Meta Ads and Google Analytics.

How do I start a project?

Use the contact page to send a short brief with your goals, timeline and references. We will come back with a suggested scope and next steps.
