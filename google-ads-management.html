Google Ads FAQ

How is Google Ads different from Meta Ads?

Google Ads reach people while they search for something, so intent is high. Meta Ads reach people while they browse, so creative matters more. Many businesses use both.

How long until I see results?

Search campaigns can start delivering clicks quickly, but we need a few weeks of data before optimizing properly. We explain what to expect before you start.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes. Tracking is the first thing we set up, so every campaign decision is based on real conversions.

Can you run Google Ads for my Shopify store?

Yes. We can connect your store, set up Shopping and Search campaigns and track purchases.
