Shopify Development FAQ

How long does it take to set up a Shopify store?

It depends on the number of products and the level of customization. We agree a clear timeline before starting.

Can you migrate or clean up my existing store?

Yes. We can restructure collections, fix the theme and tracking and optimize mobile experience on an existing store.

Do you handle product photography and 3D visuals?

We create 3D product renders and brand visuals, so your catalogue can look consistent and premium.

Will my store be set up for ads?

Yes. We install Meta Pixel and conversion tracking so campaigns can be optimized for ROAS from the start.
