Social Media Management FAQ

Which platforms do you manage?

We manage Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Tell us where your customers spend time and we will recommend where to focus.

Do I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. You see and approve the content calendar and each post before anything is published.

Do you create the designs and videos too?

Yes. Our design and motion team creates the graphics, carousels and short videos, so content stays on brand.

Can you also run paid ads?

Yes. We run Meta Ads and Google Ads, and can connect them with your organic content.
