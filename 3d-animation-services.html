3D Animation & Product Visualization FAQ

What is 3D product visualization?

3D product visualization is the creation of photoreal images and animations of a product using 3D software, instead of photographing a physical item. It lets brands show products before they exist and in any setting.

Can you animate a product from just a CAD file or sketch?

Yes. We can start from CAD files, technical drawings, reference photos or a simple sketch and rebuild the product as a clean 3D model.

Which formats do you deliver?

We deliver stills, MP4 video and social-ready cuts, sized and compressed for websites, paid ads and social platforms.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We are based in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR and work with brands and agencies worldwide, fully remotely.
