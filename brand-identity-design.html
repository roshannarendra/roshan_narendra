Brand Identity FAQ

What is brand identity design?

Brand identity design creates the visual elements that represent a business — logo, colors, typography, imagery and the rules for using them — so the brand is recognisable and consistent.

Do you design logos only, or the full identity?

Both. We can design a logo on its own, but we recommend a full identity system so the brand holds together across every channel.

Can you also create our social media and marketing assets?

Yes. We extend the identity into social templates, ad creatives and promotional visuals.

Do you rebrand existing businesses?

Yes. We refresh and modernise existing brands while keeping the equity you have built.
