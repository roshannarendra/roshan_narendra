Graphic Design FAQ

What kinds of graphic design do you do?

Social media creatives, ad banners, print collateral, packaging, presentations and event graphics. If you are not sure it fits, ask us.

Can you follow my existing brand guidelines?

Yes. We work from your brand guide, or we can build a simple one first so future designs stay consistent.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of revision rounds before starting, so the timeline and cost stay predictable.

Which files do I receive?

Export-ready files for web and social, print-ready files where needed, and editable source files on request.
