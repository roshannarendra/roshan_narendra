Motion Design FAQ

What is the difference between motion graphics and animation?

Motion graphics animate graphic elements such as text, shapes and icons, often to explain ideas. Animation is a broader term that also covers character and 3D animation. We do both.

Can you make an explainer video for a technical product?

Yes. Turning complex technical concepts into clear visual storytelling is one of our core strengths.

Do you provide videos for social media formats?

Yes. We deliver vertical, square and horizontal cuts so your video fits Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn, ads and your website.

How do we start?

Send us a short brief through the contact page with your goal, audience and reference videos you like, and we will propose an approach.
