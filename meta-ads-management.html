Meta Ads FAQ

What is ROAS?

ROAS, or return on ad spend, is the revenue earned for every unit of currency spent on ads. A ROAS of 4 means 4 earned for every 1 spent.

Do you create the ad creatives as well?

Yes. Our design and motion team creates the static, video and 3D creatives for your campaigns.

Do you need access to my Shopify store?

Access helps us set up the Pixel and conversion events correctly; we can work with the access level you are comfortable with.

Can you run ads for non-Shopify businesses?

Yes, as long as conversions can be tracked. We will confirm during the audit.
