Shopify Store Setup Checklist: 12 Steps Before You Launch

Launch ready, not just launched

A Shopify store can go live in a day, but a store that converts needs structure, testing and tracking. Use this checklist before you open the doors.

Design and theme

1. Choose and customize your theme. Match colors, fonts and layout to your brand and keep the homepage focused on one clear message.

2. Set up navigation and collections. Visitors should reach any product in two or three clicks. Group products logically and use clear menu labels.

Products and catalogue

3. Add products with strong titles and descriptions. Write for customers first, search engines second.

4. Configure variants, pricing and inventory. Sizes, colors and options should be consistent across the catalogue.

5. Use consistent, high-quality visuals. Clean photography or 3D renders on a consistent background make a catalogue look premium.

Operations

6. Set shipping zones and rates. Be transparent about cost and delivery time.

7. Configure taxes and legal pages. Add privacy, terms, refund and shipping policy pages.

8. Connect payment gateways and test checkout with a real test order.

Search and mobile

9. Optimize SEO basics. Unique page titles and meta descriptions, descriptive image alt text and clean URLs.

10. Test on mobile. Most shoppers will arrive on a phone, so check speed, tap targets and checkout flow.

Tracking and ads

11. Install Meta Pixel and conversion events (view content, add to cart, initiate checkout, purchase) so ads can optimize properly.

12. Connect Google Analytics to see traffic sources and conversion rate from day one.
